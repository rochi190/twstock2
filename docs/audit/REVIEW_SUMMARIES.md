## review:math

VERDICT: no critical defect. No look-ahead (truncation invariance exact on 264 values, features prefix-exact, forecast_latest == last walk-forward row on 136 symbols), no crash/NaN/inf/non-monotone quantile/p_up outside (0,1) on 139 real-data runs and 36 synthetic nasty series, walk-forward is bit-deterministic. The real defects are in the semantics around the maths: M-01 (ties), M-02 (oracle baseline), M-04 (calendar time), M-05 (PIT), M-06 (regime flicker).

Re-centring the predictive changes nothing (0.8865 -> 0.8866); the width multiplier needed is only +5.5%/+2.2%/+3.7% (h=1/5/20). 0050 (Kupiec p=0.013) is calendar+shock days (regular days p=0.31, excluding the 20 shock days p=0.85); 6488 (p~0) is its 2026 volatility explosion (29.9% miss in 2026).
